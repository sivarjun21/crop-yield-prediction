        What the inputs mean

        Country
        The country where the crop was grown. Farming conditions differ a lot between countries.
        Crop
        The crop the yield is estimated for, such as maize, potatoes or wheat.
        Year
        The harvest year. The model learned from records between 1990 and 2013.
        Rainfall
        How much rain the region receives in a year, in millimetres.
        Temperature
        The average yearly temperature linked to the farming region, in °C.
        Pesticides
        The amount of pesticides used in the agricultural data, in tonnes. The dataset records this for the whole country.
        Yield
        The amount of crop produced per hectare of farmland. 1 tonne per hectare is 1,000 kg per hectare. The dataset stores yield in hectograms per hectare (10,000 hg = 1 tonne), and the app converts it for you.

        Deep Learning Based Crop Yield Prediction System: an Advanced Deep Learning project. Predictions are estimates from historical data and not professional agricultural advice.
